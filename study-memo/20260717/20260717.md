```
phoneBook =
    [("betty", "555-2938")   
    ,("bonnie", "452-2928")

    ,("lucille", "205-2928")

ghci> findKey "penny" phoneBook
Just "853-2492"
ghci> findKey "betty" phoneBook
Just "555-2938"
ghci> findKey "wilma" phoneBook
```
